### 習題3-5



### 解

$$
q' = -\frac{R}{a}q
$$

$$
b = \frac{R^2}{a}
$$

$$
r^2 + b^2 -2rb\cos\theta
$$

$$
r^2 + \frac{R^4}{a^2} -2r\frac{R^2}{a}\cos\theta
$$

$$
\frac{-\frac{R}{a}q}{\sqrt{r^2 + \frac{R^4}{a^2} -2r\frac{R^2}{a}\cos\theta}} 
$$

$$
\frac{-\frac{R}{a}q}{\sqrt{\frac{R^2}{a^2}\frac{a^2}{R^2}r^2 + \frac{R^2}{a^2}R^2 -2r\frac{R^2a}{a^2}\cos\theta}} 
$$

$$
\frac{-\frac{R}{a}q}{\frac{R}{a}\sqrt{\frac{a^2}{R^2}r^2 + R^2 -2ra\cos\theta}} 
$$

$$
-\frac{q}{\sqrt{\frac{a^2r^2}{R^2} + R^2 -2ra\cos\theta}} 
$$
